Exact boundary rule

The 2D trace reflects from square walls. The 3D trace reflects from the unit sphere. The torus view uses periodic angles to map the trace onto a toroidal surface. For each axis, calculate the time until the next wall: tᵢ=(sign(vᵢ)-pᵢ)/vᵢ. Choose the nearest positive collision. At that wall, reverse only the velocity component perpendicular to the wall: vᵢ'=-vᵢ. Other velocity components are unchanged. Repeat. This is Pong-style reflection, without forces or curved motion.

2D uses a square, 3D a sphere, and the final view uses a toroidal embedding of periodic coordinates. These are continuous box-billiard traces, not the earlier repository's finite-state transition graph. The display retains only a moving window of recent segments; it does not claim mathematical completion of an infinite trajectory.
