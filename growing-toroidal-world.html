Equations and verification

State: (x,y,vx,vy). Acceleration: a(r) = -μr/(|r|² + ε²)^(3/2). Velocity-Verlet integration uses a fixed timestep. The central mass is fixed at the origin. There is no prescribed triangle, star, oscillation, or camera rotation.

The physical view shows actual simulated motion. The periodic and toroidal views are mathematical coordinate projections of that same trajectory; they do not assert that gravity acts on the torus. The projected lines break at wrap boundaries. A single softened central attraction typically gives a bounded orbit with precession, not necessarily stars. This is a demonstration, not an existing WORLD48 execution trace.
