Exact rules, projection, and completion criterion

The source is work/arc_like_maze/math.py, World.step, using phi=(0,1,2). A increments the first coordinate modulo 3. B updates the second coordinate by x₀+1, the third by phi[x₁] (when present), and each subsequent coordinate by its preceding coordinate, all modulo 3. The other coordinates remain unchanged. These are candidate finite witness rules, not a general definition of A/B.

All states are generated by breadth-first exploration from the origin. The two labeled outgoing edges of each discovered state are included, including self-loops. Lines are directed state transitions, not interpolated physical trajectories. 3D and 4D are projections; overlaps in the drawing do not identify distinct states. Completion means the reachable state set and both A/B edges for each state have been exhausted.
