Continuous oscillation, not a replay loop

Two angular coordinates evolve through coupled oscillations with distinct irrational frequency ratios. There is no hard-coded path reset. A bounded trail window follows the latest positions, and periodic crossings are retained as wrapped coordinate transitions. In exact mathematics, irrationally related frequencies can avoid an exact common period; finite browser arithmetic does not guarantee that an animated trace never revisits a location.

Geometric expansion and evidence boundary

The world expands by E₂(x,y) = (2x,2y) mod 2N. Its normalized coordinates remain unchanged as its finite resolution increases. The spherical display is not topologically equivalent to the torus; it is included as a contrasting visualization of the same coordinate inputs.
