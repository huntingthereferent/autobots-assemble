# notegpt_math_generator_v1

**Layer:** worksheet generation  
**Witness:** NoteGPT AI Math Worksheet  
**Status:** approved visual witness

## Preserve

- immediate understanding of what the tool does;
- compact, conventional generation controls;
- one obvious primary action;
- restrained SaaS polish;
- low visual noise;
- clear transition from configuration to generated artifact;
- printable result treated as the product, not as decoration.

## Do not copy

- brand identity;
- logos;
- proprietary text;
- proprietary illustrations/assets;
- exact pixel composition;
- distinctive trade dress.

## Local translation

Online School keeps the same clean generator relation but adds capabilities NoteGPT does not own in our system: curriculum provenance, learner/knowledge-goal context, saved worksheet history, editable questions and answer keys, homework handoffs, returned evidence, and next-movement generation.

Those capabilities should appear **progressively**, not all at once. The first screen should still feel like a focused worksheet generator.

## Anti-AI visual rules

No ornamental gradient by default. No glow. No fake futuristic dashboard. No random iconography. No card around every field. No oversized marketing hero within the working surface. No visual flourish unless it communicates hierarchy, state, affordance, or evidence.
