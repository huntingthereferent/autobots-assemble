# education_workspace_v1

**Layer:** education workspace  
**Status:** approved internal visual witness

## Preserve

- learner first;
- current evidenced relation before obligations;
- regrounding from observation as the primary kernel action;
- today’s activities as actuator state, not learner state;
- curriculum obligations and artifacts as separate supporting layers;
- restrained adult-facing density.

## Avoid

No gradebook-first LMS, mastery bars, streaks, badges, childish school decoration, generic AI dashboard styling, or schedule-as-path presentation.

## Local translation

The Online School home surface is an operator/learner workspace around the shared education kernel. Grade and Missouri standards constrain obligations. They do not define where the learner currently is.
