# day_attention_ops_v1

**Layer:** person day-to-day attention  
**Witness:** first internal Day-to-Day runtime surface  
**Status:** approved

## Preserve

- current attention visible without a command;
- work, Calendar, and reminders as distinct streams;
- compact operational density;
- quiet refresh;
- clear time/referent context;
- source-system boundaries.

## Avoid

No gamification, streaks, guilt language, giant productivity dashboard, ornamental AI styling, or single flattened task feed.

## Local translation

Calendar owns time relations. Day-to-Day owns attention. Personal Operations owns notes/plans/tasks. Runtime owns live handoffs. The surface may place them near each other but must not visually imply that they are one state model.
