# continuity_social_hub_v1

**Layer:** whole-ecosystem social movement  
**Status:** approved  
**Main surface:** `runtime/http/index.html`

## Preserve

- movement before application boundaries;
- one hub receiving entries from many systems;
- visible source provenance on every cross-system entry;
- reply/thread/branch/return lineage;
- time as an anchor on movement, not the page's master structure;
- quick movement actions such as reply, react, branch, handoff, resolve, and reopen;
- conversation can remain conversation until an explicit handoff creates work.

## Visual witnesses

The useful relation is shared across modern social/activity systems:

- activity feeds aggregate unlike movement while preserving type and source;
- threads keep a parent referent reachable while conversation branches;
- mentions route attention;
- reactions provide low-cost return movement;
- repost/share mechanics preserve source while opening a new audience/context;
- open discussion can later become scoped work without retroactively turning every conversation into a task.

The Continuity Layer translates those relations into a system-neutral hub rather than copying any platform's branding or page composition.

## Avoid

No school-first homepage, KPI dashboard, subsystem-card grid as the dominant structure, engagement metrics, follower mechanics, streaks, notification spam, or flattening all entries into tasks.

## Identity entrance

The main social hub may expose familiar provider entrances such as Google, Apple, GitHub, and Microsoft, but visually they are entrances into one Continuity identity rather than separate account types.

Preserve:

- one local person identity after authentication;
- provider icon/name only at the entrance and linked-account settings;
- return to the same movement front after login;
- explicit account linking rather than silent merging.

Avoid:

- a provider-branded homepage;
- treating Google/GitHub/etc. as separate user silos;
- using handles or mutable email as the durable person key;
- interrupting movement with unnecessary profile setup after an existing linked identity is resolved.

## Portal witness — Yahoo! JAPAN

Yahoo! JAPAN is a visual witness for **hub breadth**, not for copying its branding or exact layout.

Preserve the useful relations:

- one homepage can hold many unlike domains without pretending they are one application;
- global search can sit above the domain structure as a cross-system entrance;
- high-value/current modules can coexist with a broad service directory;
- personal status, calendar/time, weather/transit, news, sports, and trends can remain visually distinct while sharing one shell;
- dense information can still preserve hierarchy through columns, compact modules, headings, and predictable placement.

For Continuity, this means the homepage can combine a social/current feed with portal modules for School, Calendar, Work, Research, Factory, Jobs, and future systems rather than forcing every source into identical feed cards.

Do not copy Yahoo! JAPAN's brand marks, exact spacing, proprietary assets, or page composition. Preserve the **multi-domain portal grammar** only.
