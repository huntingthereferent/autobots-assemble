# Finite Correspondence Process

**Finite Correspondence Research** is the continuing investigation and its checkpoint evidence. The **Finite Correspondence Process** is its executable, bounded implementation. When that process receives a declared task within a workstation, its operational role is a **Correspondence Worker**.

These names distinguish **subject**, **implementation** and **assigned role**. They do not assume artificial intelligence, general autonomy, personhood, or a completed production worker.

| Name | Scope | Canonical home |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finite Correspondence Research | Mathematical questions, assumptions, checkpoints, witnesses, regressions, and provenance | [Research entry](README.md) and [checkpoint index](research/README.md) |
| Finite Correspondence Process | Executable finite operations whose capabilities are supported by research witnesses | Existing `finite_*.py`, archived integrated sources, and selected adapters |
| Correspondence Worker | One process instance/copy given a specifically admitted job | [Workspace workstation](../../../../systems/workspace/WORKSTATION.md) plus the existing handoff runtime, with a single bounded prefilter connection |

## Present execution boundary

The research includes finite route/correspondence operations, exact task requirements, selective refresh, evidence handoffs, and separate-process signature verification through CP-0030. The recovered owner-backed CP20–CP30 integrated source is now [published on `main`](research/integrated/README.md) and reconstructable from a clean checkout with checked hashes. Standalone finite references remain narrower implementations. Historical availability and selected tests do not by themselves establish a production-ready integrated worker or a full historical regression pass.

The process is not the wider language referent historically called A/B. Labelled finite `A` and `B` operators are scoped to their particular declared mathematical models. The existing `GoalAgent`, `ab_chatbot_*`, `chatbot/`, archive, and manifest identifiers remain unchanged as *historical/source identifiers*, not as the research's present name.

## In the workstation

A source tree can be copied into a **private, isolated edition** with a verifiable local file inventory and a parent version. The workstation can show which files were added, changed, or deleted and branch again without overwriting source or earlier copies.

```text
Finite Correspondence Research (evidence, source)
            |
            | explicit source selection
            v
Finite Correspondence Process (working copy)
            |
            +--> version A / experiment
            |         |
            |         +--> version B / another experiment
            |
            +--> a separate worker edition for a different bounded task
```

A copied version is **not** automatically a verified worker, a transferred mathematical witness, a registered executor, or a production deployment. To use it for a task, preserve its exact source dependencies, independently run its native tests and evidence checks, then explicitly connect it to the common [handoff runtime](../../../../runtime/README.md). Promotion or merging back to the original research requires a separate reviewed operation.
