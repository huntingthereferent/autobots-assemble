The rule is not changed after a new native result.

Before running any new action, the source enumerates EVERY state/action pair not previously observed but reachable by the known-edge graph. The separate independent verifier checks the raw pixel hashes, action order, real shared root, prior known-position prefixes and the previously unseen final predictions. It separately reports direct and delayed history results, same-edge cross-history agreement, and interrupted trials. A counterexample is saved to the artifact and fails the scientific gate rather than causing the original geometry rule to be rewritten.

This bounds the claim to the original measured LS20 map and visible glyph. It does not prove hidden-state equality, all terrains and games, a universal A/B theory, the 48-law mathematical model in native LS20, or an ARC level solved.
