## What this supports, and what remains unknown

This supports the **bounded, observed interior restoration** of pairs (1,2), (3,4), and (5,6) from the specific original stage1 root, in either tested order. It does **NOT** prove true global algebraic inverses, commutation under arbitrary native states, identity of the native hidden state (the boundary progressed after every action), equivalent scoring, an ACTION1→F3 generator mapping, or an original game solution. The six different legal native controls and distinct pixel cardinalities are source-backed evidence that the previous ACTION4/ACTION5-only 64×64 model was not the entire original action space.
