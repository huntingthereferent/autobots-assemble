## What this warrants

The experiment establishes a bounded **visible one-step congruence** at the special tile after three specific same-depth action histories and for all four native next actions. It is useful evidence that at this observed location and depth, native visible-frame equality predicts a matching next visible frame under an equal next action.

It does **not** establish that equality of visible frames anywhere implies hidden native equality, that long-term continuations cannot diverge, that mixed 0/1 terrain is uniformly passable, that the false earlier passability predicate should be revived, a full F3 correspondence, or a completed ARC game.

Next admissible experiment: predeclare two- or multi-action continuations from these same visible frames, check whether their full raw frames continue to coincide through each subsequent native action, and specifically test paths exiting and reentering the anomalous tile. Do not silently equate hidden native state with visible frame or discard the original falsification.
