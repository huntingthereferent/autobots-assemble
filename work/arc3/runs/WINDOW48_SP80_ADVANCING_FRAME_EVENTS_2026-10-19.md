## Research decision

Source-native event recording is now complete enough to distinguish genuine SDK result labels from screen-only pseudo-objectives. The combination of several real source-negative stage1 tests and this cross-stage contrast **does not provide a level2 solution**. Stop treating apparent top-row magenta contact or HUD-colored blink as an objective by themselves.

A grounded next stage1 native experiment should focus on **source bar relations and selection transitions actually observed before ACTION5**, with a *new* prospective condition and true SDK level2 outcome—not a frozen stage0 motion rule or another arbitrary bar arrangement. In every case preserve all actual returned SDK animation frames, raw original action/coordinate/level evidence, independent audit and negative outcomes. Retain separate original F3-48 working mathematics, canonical agent, historical licenses/rights unchanged.
