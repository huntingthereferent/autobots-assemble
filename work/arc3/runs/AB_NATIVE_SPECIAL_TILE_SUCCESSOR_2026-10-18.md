# A/B: prospective native successors from the same visible special tile

The prior native multi-edge experiment actually FALSIFIED the prior whole-destination color-3-or-5 passability rule. That falsification remains unchanged and its workflow stays red.

Three different native action words, 11113323, 11113332, and 33311413, ended after eight actions at the exact same full native 64x64 frame. Its frame BLAKE2B-16 is 94380564157962f5deb754fc81783a1d, and glyph position is (row 30,col 19). They also had identical native exposed status NOT_FINISHED and level counter 0. Two independently executed official LS20 original runs (39120013342 and 39120016493) yielded byte-identical complete raw evidence.

The new experiment downloads BOTH source artifacts and rechecks every original source SHA256, the three eight-action paths, individual raw source-frame metadata, matching root pixels and final full raw-frame equality. It then freezes twelve NEW trajectories: each of those three prefixes followed by one of native action IDs 1,2,3,4. Every independent trajectory resets in original official LS20.

Two independent prospective hypotheses are frozen before new native actions:

H1: from these three same full visible frames, applying the same next native action produces the same complete next native visible frame. There are four action groups, three unordered prefix-pair comparisons per action, twelve exact raw-frame comparisons.

H2: the observed native color-12 glyph either remains at (30,19) or moves exactly five pixels in the direction of the applied native action. Twelve testable one-step movement-envelope checks. The previous passability classifier is NOT assumed and exact next positions are not declared in advance.

The original native collector saves all raw frames; an independent module rechecks every 64x64 pixel hash and action event, the common original frame at step 8, raw successor SHA256 equality, native exposed state, level, trace count and actual glyph location. It reports both hypotheses separately, preserves interruptions/counterexamples and fails the scientific gate on disagreement.

The question concerns only a bounded visible-frame successor relationship at this particular observed tile and step depth. It cannot establish hidden native state identity, general full-state Markov dynamics, an F3 correspondence, or an ARC solve. This investigation has no REA dependency.
