## Native experiment

Prior independent native observations established visible equalities of prefix words 34/43 and 23/32, and visible inequality of 12/21. These are ARC action IDs, not F3 generators. Each member of every prefix pair was extended from a **fresh native reset** by the same predeclared suffix: 1, 2, 3, 4, 12, 21. This comprises 18 comparisons, 36 separately reset trajectories and **120 native environment actions**. Exact full 64x64 frame roots agreed in every run; all prior prefix relations reproduced.

| Native prefix words | suffix 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 12 | 21 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 34 versus 43 | Equal | Equal | Equal | Equal | Equal | Equal |
| 23 versus 32 | Equal | Equal | Equal | Equal | Equal | Equal |
| 12 versus 21 | Distinct | **Equal** | Distinct | Distinct | Distinct | **Equal** |

Results: **12/12** tested suffix extensions of visibly equal prefixes remained equal. There were **zero** observed splits from an equal-visible prefix. The 12/21 inequality converged after suffix 2 and after suffix 21 (**2/6**) but persisted under four other suffixes. Neither finite agreement nor visible convergence proves hidden-state equality.
