### Exact novel-coordinate falsification — prospective x=24 and x=31

At each newly reached original pre-action frontier **x=24 (word step18)** and **x=31 (word step23)**, **eight independently reset real stage1 native runs** provided original complete frame changes. Every one advanced **TWO** native color14→0 row63 pixels. ALT had predicted width2 and was correct on all 16 novel effects. The seven-pixel-extrapolation candidate guessed width1 and **missed the second changed pixel in all 16**.

Thus the tested seven-pixel spacing repair is **directly falsified** on independent, never-before-used x positions. This is stronger than a merely lower aggregate result. The earlier x10/x17 discrepancy remains real but cannot be extrapolated to x24/31 with this rule.

## Decision and next handoff

Preserve the newly witnessed ALT failure at x27 rather than patching x27 into an ever-growing exception lookup and labeling that causal knowledge.
