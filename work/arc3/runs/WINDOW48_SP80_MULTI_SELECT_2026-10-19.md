Genuine per-step legal native actions, native level, WIN/GAME_OVER, before/after source pixels, changed x/y and old/new original color, hashes and all original NPZ 64×64 frames are retained. **Native `levels_completed>=2` or explicit WIN** is the only game-progress condition.

## Decision and next bounded experimental boundary

Multi-object native selection is now **actually witnessed with correct ACTION6 coordinates**, and its order and follow-on movements are well documented from genuine original native game frames. None of the 17 fixed short source action programs completed native stage1; all remained at **1 of 6** levels. Thus selecting more than one bar and issuing ACTION5 in these sampled placements is insufficient for progression.

Future original-native work should inspect authentic screen feedback **during** ACTION5 and native multi-frame response, and test geometrically meaningful placements of multiple bars against original score/level changes. It must preserve *all* frame(s) emitted by the native SDK, not silently substitute the last visible frame for a full animation trace, and avoid interpreting source HUD-like glyphs, colored target-like regions or unexplained count changes as a WIN. Source `levels_completed>=2` and `GameState.WIN` alone establish objective progress.

Preserve previously authenticated source artifacts, historical coordinate-free ACTION6 limitations, the F3-48 original mathematical laws, canonical ARC agent, and owner rights unchanged. Do not introduce a synthetic game, a generic bot, hidden-state identity or an invented six-level competition score.
