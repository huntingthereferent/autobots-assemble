## Frontier mathematics / theory

### NEW — Millennium Prize Problems

**Lane:** Theory / Research (+ prize)  
**Field:** mathematics / mathematical physics  
**Hardness:** historic frontier problems with extremely high validation burden.  
**Reward:** CMI allocated $1,000,000 to each problem.  
**Credential gate:** no degree requirement in the prize rules.  
**Acceptance wall:** a claimed solution must be published in a qualifying outlet, wait at least two years, and receive general acceptance in the global mathematics community before CMI considers it.  
**Current nuance:** Poincaré is solved. On 2026-09-11 CMI announced Navier–Stokes had *apparently* been settled; formal evaluation/credit remains deliberately unhurried. The remaining clear unsolved set includes Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer, Hodge, P vs NP, Riemann, and Yang–Mills/mass gap.

Sources:
- https://www.claymath.org/millennium-problems/
- https://www.claymath.org/millennium-problems/rules/
- https://www.claymath.org/news/navier-stokes-announcement/
