# A/B WORLD Trial 15 — body-support movement continuity

## Original referent and controlled difference

Original WORLD15 is a source-owned finite mathematical experiment. It keeps the same input source, original 24×6 virtual world, and frictionless translational normal-reflection rule. **Only the support geometry of the moving body changes**: a radius-0.25 disc versus an axis-aligned, fixed-orientation square with half-side 0.25. The square's rotation is constrained; its actual free rigid-body torque and spin are not solved.

The pinned source commit is `65339494abaccac3c4dd20aa89439af057ec3035`. The source's independent numerical oracle is run separately, before its output is admitted to the general A/B continuity ledger.

## What moves and what gets recorded

Four source angles (0°, 7.5°, 12.25°, 20°) across both bodies produce eight original, source-witnessed, two-contact **modeled** movements. A/B retains each as a separate bounded ledger, not one global simulation. Each ledger records the declared source face, body shape and support rule, incoming movement, source contact, newly source-derived receiver, later modeled receiver contact, time-sampled trajectory and a source digest. The first modeled contact is observed in event order *before* introducing the derived receiver object and its ancestry relation.

The original square at 30° has `NO_ADMITTED_INTERIOR_CONTACT`. This is **not** a physical miss and not a solved endpoint-contact movement; a ninth ledger preserves the refusal without inventing a receiver, contact, or trajectory. Failed or unadmitted paths remain visible instead of being silently repaired.

Original test output, refusal, independent source verdict, reconstructed A/B receipts and SHA-256 manifest are retained as separate CI artifacts. Checksums preserve recorded relationships but do not authenticate physical devices or measurements. The existing per-plan resource bound is respected.

## Separate verification responsibilities

The original WORLD15 verifier recomputes finite contact support, first/second contact times and positions, face endpoints, outgoing velocities, trajectory samples, source-frame separation and the square's constrained orientation. A/B separately verifies event ancestry, chronological movement, object introduction, record integrity and the refused branch.

Negative checks refuse unsupported material claims, fabricated receiver ancestry or activation, reordered frames, relabeled body type, altered refusal status, substituted source commit and tampered ledger receipts.

Merge requires **both** the dedicated native-source/ledger workflow and the repository-wide protocol workflow to succeed on the PR head. A single fast unit-test pass is not sufficient.

## Claim boundary

This is a comparison of finite **virtual** body-support rules and the continuations they construct, not evidence of a motor, real material friction, actual contact impulse, free square rotation, new physical law, NIH equivalence or an all-purpose world solver. Original WORLD models, neutral A/B math, W13, ARC, NIH, World48, renderer and existing A/B licensing remain unchanged.
