# Trial16 — sampled rotating-square overlap is a refusal, not continued contact physics

Original WORLD16 returns to the original Trial04 rotating-square oblique
impact, rather than the fixed-orientation square of Trial15. From each
source-owned first impact it projects a short candidate ballistic position
and free angular orientation, then checks the four rotating vertices against
the **original finite source face** at 0.001-second intervals.

There are eight declared source angles and 61 finite samples per angle
over the 0.06-second window. The original source's independent numerical
oracle reports **six cases with detected sampled overlap** and **two
with no overlap detected** at these settings. A sampled overlap rejects
the unsupported projected continuation: no second impact response or
receiver object is constructed. No sampled overlap is *not* continuous
clearance, intersample nonpenetration or a proof of a future collision.

Original source ownership: pinned commit
`6d22286b1fd129ae4011d1abb55dd4c320d58a5a`, including original
first-contact evidence and independent 61-point rotating-vertex verification.
The general A/B adapter copies no collision solver.

Each of eight source cases has a separate bounded A/B history. The original
first modeled impact is preserved as an initial source object. The
postimpact rotating-square **projection** is explicitly hypothetical and
never marked as admitted dynamics. Each sample is source-reported,
including the original center, angle, worst finite-face corner and
signed clearance. The first overlapping sample is marked as such;
samples after that point are kept as counterfactual projections, **not**
further verified motion of a valid contact response.

The source's candidate receiver remains unbuilt. The A/B ledger includes
no receiver object and no completed second contact; its event observations
verify only source report provenance and bounded record consistency.
Eight independent ledger reconstructions and one ordered source-linked
manifest preserve all 488 hypothetical sample observations while respecting
the unchanged 1 MB-per-plan capacity.

This is not an instrument measurement of Earth-material behavior, a
continuous rotating polygon solver, a solved recontact impulse, or an
automatic construction of a next face. The next source-owned contact test
must investigate the observed failure/refusal; it cannot silently promote
the false ballistic projection into physically valid motion.
