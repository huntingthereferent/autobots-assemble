starting spin `{0, 2}`. For 35 previously fixed source/controlled
geometries it produces **420** attempted single-active candidates.

finite-horizon candidates** and **44 rejected candidates** at pinned commit

its independent friction, spin, energy and finite geometry oracle remain

The native source JSON is preserved in full, as are the 35 separately
reverified A/B receipts. An ordered source-hashed manifest binds the
