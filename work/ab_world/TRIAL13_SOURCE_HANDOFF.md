# Trial13 — conditional coupled mode equations, not a measured frictional collision

The original WORLD Trial13 source constructs local simultaneous, two-contact
frictional mode *hypotheses*, rather than silently replacing the original
frictional Trial06–07 fail-closed stop. It checks source-owned coupled
Jacobian equations for normal and tangential impulses under frictionless,
stick and directional-slip assumptions; admissibility also requires finite
energy, unilateral contact, continuous clearance and complete 31-frame
virtual movement.

At pinned original commit `6179d4e6b6e66a02065e8a5c1ef64b855a2de763`,
35 original source/controlled-angle cases cover **1,330** mode attempts.
The original independent source and Chromium witnesses reported **60
admissible conditional modes and 1,270 rejections**. Admissible counts by
spin and friction in predeclared source order: 18, 0, 0, 18, 12 and 12.
This is not verification of a real frictional simultaneous collision.

The general A/B adapter uses the original model results as source-labelled
movement records only. Each of the 35 source cases becomes a separately
bounded world under the unchanged 1 MB plan limit. All group definitions,
mode identities, acceptance/rejection statuses, attempted source impulses,
full-horizon eligibility and per-case provenance remain preserved.

Each admissible source mode carries every one of its 31 time-ordered position,
velocity, orientation and spin frames as independent revisions. Each rejected
mode preserves its original reason and, where present, the unsuccessful
attempted impulse; it never gains an outgoing material velocity or a
completed virtual trajectory. Independent A/B reconstruction checks all 35
case ledgers and hashes, with an ordered source-digest manifest.

This adapter does not own collision equations, choose a material contact
order, verify instruments, prove general frictional manifolds or produce a
physical blueprint. The original WORLD source and its independent native
checker are used unchanged to establish the limited *model* claims.
The declared original two-contact frictional source remains unresolved.

No original WORLD, W13, NIH, ARC, World48, renderer, general-world
construction rules or All Rights Reserved license has been changed.
