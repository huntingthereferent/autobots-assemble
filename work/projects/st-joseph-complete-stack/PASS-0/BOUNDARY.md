# PASS-0 Evidence Boundary

## Purpose

Freeze what may orient the first representation before deeper reality-check evidence is opened.

This pass is intended to test whether Complete Stack can recover a useful dependency structure from bounded public evidence rather than simply copying a complete existing city model.

## Admitted in PASS-0

Only broad, public, high-level evidence:

- City of St. Joseph Comprehensive & Land Use Plan overview;
- City transportation/planning project index;
- City Public Works & Transportation responsibility description;
- City Utility Services provider/service listing;
- U.S. Census Bureau QuickFacts for population, household/economic, and digital-access context;
- City homepage/community high-level attributes where useful.

See `evidence/sources.json`.

## Deliberately withheld from construction of the first landscape

These are not forbidden forever. They are held back so later comparison is meaningful.

- parcel-level GIS;
- detailed utility network geometry;
- engineering drawings;
- detailed road geometry and intersection inventories;
- traffic counts and detailed crash distributions;
- bridge/road condition inventories;
- sewer/stormwater asset inventories;
- detailed airport asset geometry;
- building-by-building inventories;
- detailed municipal budgets and capital schedules;
- private utility operating data;
- real-time operational telemetry;
- field measurements;
- detailed aerial/photogrammetric reconstruction;
- component-level dimensions/capacities unless a later branch explicitly admits them.

## PASS-0 witness level

The landscape is **represented**, not measured.

It witnesses:

- named system classes present in the admitted evidence;
- coarse dependencies and interfaces justified by those sources;
- explicit external-provider boundaries;
- places where later decomposition should open.

It does not witness:

- exact system geometry;
- current asset condition;
- capacity;
- reliability;
- performance;
- detailed ownership of every asset;
- completeness of every subsystem.

## Freeze rule

When PASS-0 begins producing quantitative or spatial predictions, preserve them before opening withheld evidence.

Do not silently revise PASS-0 after comparison.
