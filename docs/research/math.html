Exact transformation and parameter definitions
Coordinates mod 3. A(u,v,z) = (u+1, v, z) B(u,v,z) = (u, v+u+1, z+q(u,v)) q = [u=2 AND v<k] + e·[u=0 AND v=0] + t·([u=1 AND v=0] - [u=1 AND v=1]) + c·((v+u+1)²-v²)

k ∈ {0,1,2,3}; e,t ∈ {0,1}; c ∈ {0,1,2}. The displayed finite calculation follows this formula rather than an animated or inferred physical trajectory.
