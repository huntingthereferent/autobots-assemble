# Support the research

## How this began

I didn't start out trying to build a software product. I started by trying to communicate more clearly with the people around me.

I used AI conversations to examine where our meanings diverged. I noticed that I could repeatedly identify and correct mismatches between what I meant and what the system understood. That led me to look more closely at the relationships I was tracking, and to develop shorthand for referring to them.

The investigation expanded into mathematical constructions and tests. I began recording predictions, outcomes, counterexamples, and source evidence so the work could be examined rather than merely described.

Eventually, chat windows became independent work lanes. Those lanes developed into Tracework: an experimental environment for organizing finite working contexts, preserving handoffs, and keeping research results connected to their sources.

The work is ongoing. It includes mathematical experiments, ARC investigations, conditional-world tests, source-data comparisons, and tools for inspecting the records.

## Why support it?

I'm an independent researcher, working outside an institution. This project is a public record of an investigation that began with a human problem and grew into software and mathematical research.

Voluntary support would help my household make room for rest and continued research. It isn't a purchase, a promise of private access, or a subscription to future deliverables. There is no expectation that anyone contribute.

If you find the work interesting and want to help it continue, you're welcome to support it. Thank you for taking the time to look.
