Finite World — Live Trace

A discrete toroidal grid with two defined moves. Every transition is computed, recorded and checked. This illustrates bounded search, not a universal solver or a smooth-manifold proof.

A:(x,y)→(x+1,y) mod 17; B:(x,y)→(x,y+1) mod 17. The prescribed action sequence A,A,B,A,B,B repeats. Wraparound is a property of the defined world.
