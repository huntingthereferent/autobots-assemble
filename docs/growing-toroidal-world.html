What the animation demonstrates

The torus is a three-dimensional visualization of a two-dimensional periodic coordinate system. Moving past one boundary returns through the opposite boundary. The trace remains on the surface while the world expands from N to 2N.

The luminous trajectory represents a deliberately defined example path. It is not a claim that nearby points share meaning, provenance, or native source relations.
