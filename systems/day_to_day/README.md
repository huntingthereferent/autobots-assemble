# Day-to-Day

Day-to-Day is the person-facing continuity layer.

It does not own Education, Online School, Personal Operations, companies, or other systems. Those systems keep their own state and may emit handoffs to a person.

Day-to-Day adds only explicit recurring attention:

```text
live handoffs assigned to person
+
explicit recurring reminders for person
→ current attention projection
```

Activation words are entrances into that projection. A scheduler/notification surface can call the same projection without changing its meaning.

## Boundary

- Do not infer obligations from notes, history, household roles, or identity.
- A reminder exists only after explicit admission.
- A source system remains the authority for its own work.
- Day-to-Day may remind; it does not silently complete, reschedule, or reinterpret source work.
- Different people can have different activation words and different projections.

This is intentionally separate from the education kernel. Education may emit a handoff for a person; Day-to-Day only helps that person keep it in front when it needs attention.
