# Education Kernel

This is the smallest executable form of the education kernel.

It does not begin from grade, age, name, curriculum position, or a prior learner profile. Those may be context in another layer. The kernel regrounds on evidence attached to the person and referent currently in front of it.

```text
Observation
→ reconcile with current learner relation
→ new current learner relation

behind:
observation + prior relation remain reachable as natural provenance
```

A later observation may contradict an earlier relation. The earlier relation is preserved; it simply stops being the current front.

## Human learning baseline

Education observes two related movements:

1. knowledge becoming available to the person;
2. the person's ability to intentionally learn under an imposed requirement.

Modern education frequently begins with an external obligation:

```text
external requirement
→ person must hold an assigned referent
→ person intentionally directs attention / movement toward it
→ evidence returns
→ knowledge relation and force-learning relation may both change
```

The external requirement does not define the learning path.

```text
curriculum / law / standard
= what must become reachable

person in front
= what is actually available now

education
= derive movement between them
```

### Knowledge relation

Can the person actually distinguish, relate, use, explain, transfer, or otherwise reach the knowledge?

### Force-learning relation

Can the person intentionally move toward something they did not choose because an external requirement makes the movement necessary?

These relations may differ.

A person may know the underlying relation while being unable to independently perform the imposed learning form.

A person may also perform the imposed form successfully without the knowledge relation surviving outside that form.

Therefore:

```text
compliance ≠ knowledge
task completion ≠ knowledge
successful forced performance ≠ ownership of relation
```

The kernel observes both without assuming either is a defect:

```text
person
+
current knowledge relation
+
current force-learning relation
+
external knowledge obligation
↓
reachable movement
↓
what happened?
↓
reground
```

## Invariants

> **The obligation may be forced. The path is still derived from the person.**

> **Education must distinguish learning the required knowledge from learning how to intentionally learn under requirement.**

The kernel intentionally does not yet choose lessons, worksheets, standards, or schedules. Those are later movements that can consume the current relation.
