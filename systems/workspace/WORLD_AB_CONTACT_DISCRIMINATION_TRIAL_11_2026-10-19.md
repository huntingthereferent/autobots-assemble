# WORLD / Trial 11 — necessary friction inequality versus missing measurement

Trial 10 independently verified 35 controlled cases: 26 had more than one locally admissible response; 9 had one under the explicitly *frictionless*, `e=1` candidate model. It did not measure which impact occurred.

Trial 11 **does not change any existing source program or claim to recover real contact history**. It tests two independent additions to Trial 10's **original retained candidate set**:

1. **Necessary static Coulomb cone only:** `|J_t| <= 0.4 J_n`. All parent candidates were derived with **zero tangential impulse** because the model was constructed with `mu=0`. Consequently they trivially satisfy this inequality at `mu=0.4`. This is **not sufficient** to construct a frictional impact law: a sliding contact may require a nonzero opposing tangential impulse, rotation, coupled normal changes and a different constitutive law. Do not count zero filtered candidates as success at solving friction.
2. **Extra all-touching-normal restitution postulate:** impose perfect reflection on both source normals even when only one is marked active. This deliberately adds an unmeasured equality. Record which existing candidate responses it eliminates, but do not represent the postulate as an observed physical law, nor silently replace a unilateral contact with an impossible negative impulse.

A third question concerns **missing independent evidence**. For each case and candidate, derive *hypothetical* outgoing positions from the 31 previously verified motion frames, and group them into positional quantization bins at times `0.40, 0.60, 1.10` with grid spacings `0.1,0.01,0.001` virtual world units. A sensor is *potentially discriminating* when each candidate maps to a distinct bin. This is an experiment-design calculation **only**. No real measurement is present, no value may be installed in the receipt, and no active set may be chosen from a hypothetical partition.

Preserve original Trial 09 provenance for counterfactual rotated segment B, original Trial 06 `mu=0.4` unresolved baseline, and the original WORLD perimeter. Test all 35 cases, all 9 sensor choices, every candidate and full provenance with an independent native oracle. In real Chromium, load the unmodified original Trial 10 interactive source as a same-origin frame, run its actual Trial 09 subprocess, recalculate the counterfactual discrimination grid with independent JS, and compare complete receipts against Python before reporting a result.

This trial is not a friction model, a selected material event, a sequential impact witness, a physically built blueprint, or proof of a unique collision law.
