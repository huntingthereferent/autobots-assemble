# WORLD Trial 30 — bounded local separation after an unselected fourth-response hypothesis

Original WORLD Trial29 independently qualified three mathematical contact-response candidates using the same finite source-face geometry and frictionless, restitution-1 rigid-square rule used in earlier original trials. It did not witness a physical fourth impact, select the law as material reality or establish any movement after the response.

Trial30 tests one new, narrower question: **if the proposed outgoing linear and angular velocities were taken as a conditional source movement, would the existing 24×6 WORLD remain locally separated from both existing finite-face supporting planes and its four perimeter planes over the next 0.008 virtual-time units?**

For each of the three qualified original cases, retain the same source contact center, orientation, square corner identity, original normal, and candidate outgoing velocity and spin. No geometry or receiver is added. For each of the square's four corners, calculate signed gap and outgoing normal rate at the conditional contact relative to the two prior finite-face supporting lines and four perimeter planes: 24 named constraints. An upper bound on normal second derivative is the square's rotation radius times angular speed squared. For the one qualified touching vertex, require its initial gap near zero and a *positive lower bound on its outward normal speed* throughout the full interval. All other corners require a positive lower bound on signed gap over the whole interval.

Every inequality is sufficient, not necessary, to establish separation under this nominal rigid-body continuation. If any of the constraints fails, Trial30 labels that conditional case **unresolved**. A failed halfplane test does not establish material intersection of a finite segment, nor does a successful floating-point test formally certify real-number or real-world clearance.

## Independent witness and source controls

The independent verifier first reruns the original WORLD29 finite contact / impulse source witness, then independently reconstructs each corner's rotation and trajectory in world coordinates, each original constraint's normal and anchor, the initial gap, outgoing normal rate, curvature envelope and time-extremum bounds. It challenges every accepted/rejected constraint, verifies one unique original touching source corner, source relationship identity, candidate response flags, and no-impulse controls. Additional sample checks try to falsify any claimed positive bounds at several interior times; these are not substitutes for the mathematical envelope.

No fourth impact is authenticated, and no fifth impulse, post-contact chain of collisions or movement beyond the bounded conditional interval is solved. Neither a new physics engine nor outward-rounded formal interval arithmetic is claimed.

Preserve all 24 individual source bounds and refusals in the native artifact.
