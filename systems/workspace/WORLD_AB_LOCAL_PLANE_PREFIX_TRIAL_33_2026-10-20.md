The signed gap along a fixed source normal is Lipschitz in time with constant bounded by

`L = |center_velocity dot normal| + |angular_speed| * square_vertex_radius`.

For positive gaps at both endpoints, the gap neighborhoods from the left and right samples cover the whole interval when their **sum exceeds the maximum possible gap change over that interval** by a positive numerical guard.
