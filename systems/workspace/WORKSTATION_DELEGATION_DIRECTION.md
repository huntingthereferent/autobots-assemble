# Workstation purpose and bounded delegation — stable continuation reference

**Why this exists:** James and ChatGPT are building several connected things at once. Language Referent / A/B is the ongoing inquiry into relationships, not a task manager or worker scheduler. The workstation is a support tool: allow the assistant to remain in conversation with James while bounded helpers execute source-owned jobs. Preserve the links among work rather than turning every session into an audit of the harness.

## Delegate instead of personally doing every bounded job

The EXISTING ProcessWorkstation owns source editions; the EXISTING HandoffRuntime and RuntimeJournal carry handoffs and history. B2's WorkstationContinuationHarness carries independently supported branch/connection observations. branch_help assigns a **registered bounded source-owned worker**. B3's WorkstationNativeFeedback invokes controls.run_job, independently checks the source/result through branch_help.verify_return, and records output evidence on the same original continuation lane. B4 may use that outcome for further independently supported movement.

**Bounded worker != AI assistant.** A bounded worker gets an exact native contract with source, inputs, scope, permission and verification. An AI helper receives open investigative context and can explore uncertain relations; model-backed AI assistants are not established by this bounded worker path. Do not conflate them.

## What counts as success

- The helper's distinct job ID and source-edition ancestry exist in the original journal.
- A native subprocess performed the W13 world sequence; the returned receipt was independently replayed against preserved W12 rules, and the job is COMPLETE.
- B3 records one return bound to its original lane and output/evidence digests; replay verifies without rerunning the subprocess or spawning a second job.
- The independent other lane remains unchanged. Evidence is retained across restarts.
- Invalid source bytes, missing witness, ambiguous assignment, invalid journal, unregistered helper or absent exact execution authority stop the movement. Unresolved walls stay visible.

**Never replace a user's work with repeated harness tests.** Regression tests protect the handoff, but the objective is working delegation so the shared investigations, games, software, research and writing can continue. After checking one real delegated result, return to the actual work and observations rather than declaring another infrastructure checkpoint to be the goal.
