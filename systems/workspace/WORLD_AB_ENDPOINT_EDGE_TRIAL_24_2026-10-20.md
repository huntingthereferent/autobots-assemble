# WORLD Trial 24 — finite source endpoints against moving square edges

Trial22 found conditional rotating-vertex crossings against supporting lines. Trial23 expanded the source search to **every inward vertex/line root** in the same bounded interval and, in the observed eight-case source, found no later root beyond Trial22's first-per-pair records.

That leaves a distinct finite geometry relationship untested: an **endpoint of a finite source segment meeting the interior of a rotating square edge**. A segment endpoint can intersect a square edge before any square vertex crosses that segment's supporting line. The original WORLD 24×6 virtual domain, two finite source faces, four-corner square and Trial21 *unselected* third impulse are unchanged.

## Bounded experiment

For each of six source-admitted third-impulse cases, WORLD24 checks 16 pairings: two endpoints of each of the two existing finite faces against the four actual rotating square edges. The prior two controls do not have an admitted third impulse and do not acquire later geometry.

The model projects from `t=0.008`, the end of Trial21's separately checked local separation, through `t=0.25` virtual time after the third hypothetical impulse. The original first vertex-line records from Trial22 remain visible and are compared with the earliest qualified endpoint/edge feature.

For each endpoint and edge, the signed cross product of the rotating edge direction and fixed endpoint displacement identifies *outside-to-inside* crossings of that edge's supporting line. A 4,096-step numeric sign sweep with bisection records crossing candidates. Each is classified by its fractional position on the **actual finite square edge**: interior, beyond the edge extension, or unresolved at a square vertex. A candidate earlier than the prior vertex-line event remains only a **candidate**, not a chosen fourth impact.
