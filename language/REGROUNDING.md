These are James's exact words, not edited quotations. The observation about how people used to talk is his framing, not an independently established historical claim.

The correction was to the idea of *fixing* communication. People already move between conversation, activity, memory, feeling, humor, disagreement and technical questions. More available subjects and surfaces can allow a conversation to range farther, sometimes farther than its participants wanted. James calls the useful response **regrounding**. Surfing is his image for it: move with the conversation while keeping enough orientation to recover what is being followed. It is not a demand to stop the movement or travel only in a straight line.

## The continuing relationship

**The language referent** is the same referent earlier called **A/B**. A changed name does not introduce a second thing. A stable name helps us return to a relationship without freezing it into one statement, one shape ID, or one finite mathematical model.

When James says he *swallows* observations, he is not necessarily asking to merge software layers. Observation branches outward through other people, settings and silences; several encounters can become relevant together. A person can learn from a different way of playing a game without correcting anyone. The relation might only become apparent somewhere else. It is not a sequence of isolated comparisons against himself.

Neither speech nor silence is automatically the solution. Each can help or hurt depending on the relationship and what happens next. Laughter, emotion, swearing, hesitation, playfulness, and precision can all belong to the same person. We should not convert an ordinary interaction into a technical proof, or a precise technical correction into a personality change.

## What regrounding does

A useful return keeps these distinctions available, without treating them as mandatory conversational stages:

1. **Where were we?** Recover the person or project's actual referent and the relationship being followed.
2. **Where did we travel?** Keep the branch, observation, correction, or change in perspective; don't erase it merely because it wasn't the intended destination.
3. **What is still connected?** Name what survived, what changed, and what may have become uncertain. A familiar word alone is not proof of the same meaning.
4. **Where do we continue?** Resume from the recovered relationship. Another branch remains possible; the return isn't a command to stop exploring.

In casual conversation this may take one sentence: *No, I meant regrounding, not fixing people.* In mathematics it may require checking whether an explicitly specified correspondence really preserves an operation. The activity differs; the referent is not split into a "human" version and a "mathematical" version.

## A question is already participation

If someone asks, **"Isn't that math?"**, the question has already brought mathematics and the conversation into relation. Mathematics is a human formal language. It does not have to be attached afterward before a relationship can be discussed. A formal equation can still make an exact, testable claim under declared assumptions, which ordinary conversation does not automatically establish.

James's wording matters:

> Yes yes names are important so we can study them and not keep fucking up

Names help people return, correct and study what they are following. They don't certify an interpretation. When a name or a mathematical model starts replacing the relationship it was meant to express, reground **the same referent** rather than inventing a new one.

## Across the repository

- **Human conversation and account:** preserve original utterances, emotional context, timing and humor; identify any AI-authored paraphrase as paraphrase.
- **English decoding:** retain context and multiple possible interpretations. "Swallow" in a conversation about observation is not automatically the operator `LAYER.ABSORB`; "surfing" is not automatically a command to execute a movement.
- **Pragmatics and rendering:** changing register may not quietly clean up James's actual voice or strengthen what he claimed.
- **Words movement:** a branch or silence is a record of an interaction, not proof of its intention or social effect.
- **Handoffs:** carry the referent, the route that brought us here, genuine uncertainty, and the return target. Regrounding can preserve a detour rather than deleting it.
- **Mathematics and engineering:** use native definitions, proofs, tests and witnesses for concrete results. A finite model is a specified expression within the inquiry, not a substitute name for the whole of it.
- **Provenance:** a witnessed result, self-report, hypothesis, interpretation and paraphrase must remain distinguishable.

The documents under `language/` describe these different surfaces. They do **not** claim to implement general human understanding, authenticate someone's identity from prose, or prove a universal mechanism of language.

**Same James. Same referent. Different moments and levels of precision. Reground where needed; keep surfing.**
