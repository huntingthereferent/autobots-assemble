## The question

**Human Drift** names the subject James brought into focus on October 13. The phrases he used before that date are not competing names for a newly invented mechanism. They are **a trail of attempts to keep one continuing language referent available while a conversation—and often the AI's answer—moved away from what he meant**.

This is the important difference between a dictionary of synonyms and a provenance record. James was not always trying to give the same description in interchangeable words. He was observing, changing vantage, correcting an assistant's misunderstanding, taking in what the detour showed, and speaking again. The corrections themselves belong to the evidence.

His later shorthand:

> yes and than you showing how many words i used for it since sept etc.... i was moving around your missunderstand essentially

The quote above and all other blockquotes attributed to James here preserve the supplied conversational spelling and punctuation. Dates identify the conversations; the explanatory prose is AI-authored interpretation. The record does not independently verify a general psychology or universal mathematics of human drift.

## What is being studied

A conversation can travel through different subjects, people, experiences and formal descriptions while participants attempt to preserve a relationship. Sometimes one person continues following it while another follows only the latest label. Their explanations can then drift apart despite apparently agreeing on the words.

James's outward observation includes people's perspectives, silences and actions beyond any one pair of examples. His word *swallow* describes taking those observations in together, so a new encounter may change what becomes visible in an older one. He does not need to correct every person whose approach differs from his; a difference may be precisely what he learns from.

**Regrounding** is not necessarily rewinding to the first utterance. It means bringing the new observations back into relation with what was previously being followed, seeing what survives and what needs correction. A later account is allowed to develop rather than mechanically repeat an earlier sentence.

**Human Drift** here is a proposed subject of investigation grounded in James's reported experience and these observable conversational corrections. No measure, psychological law, universal period of oscillation, or automatic algorithm is claimed from the quotations.

## The same person through the record
