# Human Drift — Technical Scope

[Language overview](README.md) · [Regrounding](REGROUNDING.md) · [Evidence contract](../LANGUAGE_REFERENT_CONTRACT.md)

Human Drift is a working subject for examining when a continuing conversational or observational relationship changes under new descriptions, corrections, and context. It is not a finite operator, a measured psychological law, or a claim that different speakers share one internal process.

## Observable boundaries

- Identify the present referent and the context in which an expression was made.
- Distinguish an observed utterance or action from a proposed interpretation.
- If an interpretation proves mistaken, record the correction without inventing a stronger original claim.
- A branch may produce useful observations; those observations still need their own sources and limits.
- For software or mathematical tests, use native actions, state, conditions, and witnesses.

This technical note does not republish personal conversational excerpts or a retired quotation chronology. The currently published [writing archive](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/huntingthereferent/tree/main/writing) is the public writing index; withdrawn material must not be reconstructed from old citations or historical revisions.
