# Reading and Referent — Evidence Boundary

[Language overview](README.md) · [Pragmatics](PRAGMATICS.md)

An expression can affect how a reader understands or feels about a continuing exchange without establishing that the underlying referent objectively changed.

A first-person report is evidence of what its speaker reported, not an instrument measurement of another person's experience. Claims about mechanisms, diagnoses, universality, or mathematical equivalence need independent evidence.
