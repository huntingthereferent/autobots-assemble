# Source Witnesses

This layer uses platform mechanics as evidence for recurring social movement relations. It does not copy platform taxonomies as the canonical model.

- X Help: conversations begin from posts and continue through replies; mentions target attention; reposts and quote posts carry a referent into another audience/context.
- Reddit Help: posts establish roots, comments/replies create nested discussion, and repost/crosspost carries a referent into another community.
- Slack Help: mentions target attention, threads branch discussion around a message, and reactions/activity provide lower-cost signals and notification surfaces.
- Discord Help: mentions and unread/reply surfaces route attention; reactions and message navigation preserve lightweight return paths.
- YouTube Help: comments and replies create audience conversation around a post/video.
- GitHub Discussions: discussions support open conversation, Q&A/answers, decisions, and transition into more scoped work such as issues.

These witnesses justify the cross-platform movement bank; they are not evidence that every platform exposes every anchor directly.
