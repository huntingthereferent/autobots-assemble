# Work Protocol — Job Hunt

## Referent

A concrete external opportunity, not the general idea of making money.

## Admission

A listing enters the work queue only when the source, scope, observable acceptance condition, and current availability can be represented without inventing hidden requirements.

## Preferred ordering

Prioritize work that is:

1. bounded;
2. locally testable;
3. software/data/research compatible;
4. explicit about acceptance;
5. buildable without hidden access.

Hidden-client dependencies are not disqualifying, but they must become explicit walls rather than guessed requirements.

## Output

Create a canonical work record with source, referent, boundary, acceptance, evidence, status, wall(s), and next movement.
