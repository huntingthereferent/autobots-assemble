# Language Referent — Evidence Contract

## Continuity and finite representations

Language, meanings, and relationships are already changing. James describes their continuity through **toroidal movement**: movement, the relations being followed, and the changing space belong together, rather than a fixed container followed by a separate motion stage.

A finite toroidal model is a **witness**, not the referent. Its bounded wraparound coordinates support tracing a specified movement, but keeping the relationship identifiable also requires source identity, transition and relation-update rules, observations, and provenance. The **recursion test** checks whether the output remains eligible as the next input under the same evidence conditions, with unresolved differences retained. The test concerns the declared model or operation; it does not independently establish universal truth.

The operational diagram below describes an **evidence and admission boundary**, not the geometry of continuity. Its arrows are not a claim that continuity is a linear pipeline. For the distinction between a finite wraparound grid, a smooth torus, and tested compatibility of movement under changes in resolution, see [the shape contract](language/SHAPE_CONTRACT.md).

Tracework is an incomplete, evolving research and implementation environment. A finite model's unproved correspondence to the wider referent is an open research boundary, not automatically a software defect. Duplicated code, invalid claims, and failed tests remain implementation issues that can and should be corrected. Preserve the difference.

## What the language referent is

**Name continuity:** "the language referent" and the earlier name "A/B" designate the **same referent** in James's account. The newer name is a communication choice for readers, not a new layer, replacement, or separate thing that later acquires mathematics. Mathematics is human formal language within the continuing investigation. The finite models remain scoped representations with their own proof obligations. See the [research account](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/huntingthereferent) for the current public research record.

**Language Referent** names the continuing relationship being followed while its descriptions change. The name supplies language for recognizing, describing, and continuing relationships in movement without replacing those relationships with words. The referent is the relationship through the step; it is not itself a theorem, equation, notation system, or a specific mathematical operation. See [Language Referent](LANGUAGE_REFERENT.md) for the current account, [the October 13 grounding note](LANGUAGE_REFERENT_GROUND_2026-10-13.md) for the naming and regrounding corrections, and [the October 11 historical record](AB_Ground_Record_Current_Note_2026-10-11.md) for the earlier physical two-step hypothesis.

Mathematics is a human formal language. Particular generators formally labeled A and B, inverse constructions, and correspondence proofs in this repository are **formal mathematical representations** of specified relations, with their own state spaces, assumptions, witnesses, and scope. Do not mistake a result proved for a particular finite transformation family for a definition or universal proof of the language referent.

This repository does **not** redefine the language referent as a workflow engine, project manager, policy engine, state machine, job tracker, filesystem layout, or AI prompt.

## Using the language referent in repository operations

Operational layers must respect these invariants:

1. **Current referent is primary.** Older layers are evidence only when the current relation actually reaches them.
2. **Movement requires a witness/evidence contract.** A relation is not admitted merely because it is convenient or plausible.
3. **Outputs are checked for re-entry.** A completed movement can become input on the next pass only when the same contract's evidence and witness requirements remain satisfied; otherwise record the wall or unresolved difference.
4. **Walls are explicit.** Missing evidence, missing authority, inaccessible tools, unresolved correspondence, or incompatible contracts produce a wall rather than a fabricated bridge.
5. **UNKNOWN/PARTIAL are preserved.** Lack of evidence is not evidence of absence, and coverage is not automatically preservation.
6. **Provenance survives movement.** Source, witness, parent relation(s), and the reason for movement remain recoverable.
7. **No silent reorientation.** An implementation detail, old experiment, or prior conclusion cannot replace the current referent unless a supported movement reaches it.
8. **Another side, same referent.** A blocked relationship may be inspected from another vantage; this does not license a new referent or bypass the witness conditions.

```text
CURRENT REFERENT
      +

      +

      |
      v
MOVE / WALL / UNKNOWN / PARTIAL
      |
      v

```

The protocol is an application boundary. It is **not the language referent's language-level definition**, a universal mathematical result, or a verified physical two-step.

## Recursion test

A protocol is structurally acceptable only if its output can be used as input to another run of the same protocol shape without inventing a new evidence standard.

That is the practical closure test used throughout this repository.

## Returning from a branch

The [Human Drift technical note](language/HUMAN_DRIFT.md) describes the boundaries for tracking changing descriptions and corrections; it is not a published conversation chronology. Preserve documented source boundaries and technical corrections, without restoring retired personal quotations. [Regrounding](language/REGROUNDING.md) is a conversational correction and orientation practice, not an extra mathematical operator or a promise that a relation always returns. Retain an observed branch, correct a mistaken interpretation, and resume the **same** referent if evidence allows. A human conversation may reground informally; a finite mathematical continuation still needs its specific witness.

One person, many registers: James's humor, directness, feeling and technical questions do not imply different author identities. When attribution matters, use currently published authorized sources; never reconstruct withdrawn quotations or present AI paraphrase as speech.
