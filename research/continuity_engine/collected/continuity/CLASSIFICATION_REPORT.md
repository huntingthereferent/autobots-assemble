## 1. Current mathematical referent

Let the state space be exactly \(X=\mathbb F_3^5\) (243 states), with \(x=(u,v,z,y,t)\). All coordinates are modulo three. We retain the same first four-coordinate rules:

\[
A(u,v,z,y,t)=(u+1,v,z,y,t),
\]
\[
B_q(u,v,z,y,t)=(u,\;v+u+1,\;z+[v=2],\;y+[v=2\land z=2],\;t+q(z,y)).
\]

The earlier 702-law subfamily imposed \(q(z,y)=a_z+b_zy\), with \(b\neq0\). This investigation removes that additional restriction: \(q:\mathbb F_3^2\to\mathbb F_3\) can be *any* nine-entry table. Therefore there are exactly \(3^9=19,683\) laws. The mathematical carrier remains five-dimensional; it has **not** been expanded to accommodate the larger law family.

## 2. Explicit positive transports

Two state-space bijections remain valid **for all 19,683 coupling laws**:

\[
R(u,v,z,y,t)=(u,v,z+1,y+[z=2],t),
\qquad
S(u,v,z,y,t)=(u,v,z,y,2t).
\]

The transported couplings are determined without searching for an isomorphism:

\[
(Rq)(z+1,y+[z=2])=q(z,y),\qquad (Sq)(z,y)=2q(z,y).
\]

Both transports preserve the complete labeled A/B action: \(R\circ A=A\circ R\), \(R\circ B_q=B_{Rq}\circ R\), and likewise for \(S\). They commute, \(R^9=S^2=\mathrm{id}\), and produce the \(C_9\times C_2\) positive-transport group.

Direct independent arithmetic verified **9,565,938 B-generator intertwining equations** (19,683 laws × 243 states × 2 transforms); the A equations, which do not depend on \(q\), were also checked on every state. Equivalent to 19,131,876 generator comparisons if the law-independent A checks were repeated for every coupling.

## 3. Four linked orbit-incidence invariants prove there are no other mergers

For a finite generator word \(w\in\{A,a,B,b\}^*\), with lowercase denoting inverses and words applied **left to right**, let \(O_w(x)\) denote the cyclic orbit generated by repeated application of \(w\). For words \(p,q,r\), define

\[
J_{p,q;r}(x)=|O_p(x)\cap O_q(r(x))|.
\]

A simultaneous conjugacy \(H\) transports both orbits and their linked roots. Hence the **histogram over all 243 states** of \(J_{p,q;r}\) is an exact conjugacy invariant. A histogram mismatch proves nonconjugacy. A match alone does **not** generally prove conjugacy.

Four specific features are sufficient for this declared family:

| Feature | Linked incidence \(J_{p,q;r}\) | Distinct cumulative fingerprints among the 1,098 R/S orbits |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | \(J_{BAB,Ab;A}\) | 476 |
| 2 | \(J_{ABab,ABBabbbb;B}\) | 1,048 |
| 3 | \(J_{ABA,ABBabbbb;AB}\) | 1,082 |
| 4 | \(J_{ABBabbbb,ABBB;b}\) | **1,098** |

The values were recomputed for the 1,098 canonical positive-orbit representatives using direct full-state permutations and bitset orbit operations. An earlier implementation of the original four-feature catalog agreed with an independent direct permutation implementation for every representative. All 2,196 representative R/S fingerprint transports were replayed, with no mismatches.

**Proof for this finite family:** Every law belongs to one of 1,098 R/S positive-transport orbits. Each orbit has a valid constructed full-A/B conjugacy connecting all members. The four-part histograms are pairwise distinct across all 1,098 orbits and are invariant under any full-A/B conjugacy. Consequently no conjugacy can merge different orbits. Therefore the full unrooted A/B conjugacy relation has exactly **1,098 classes**.

The four features were found by refining an earlier nine-feature experiment. Exhaustive testing of all subsets of those nine discovered features found that **four is the smallest sufficient size within that nine-feature catalog**; no global minimum or arbitrary-system theorem is asserted.

## 4. Independent negative-check route, without the new complete fingerprints

As a separate verification, the **original four** observations from the 702-law investigation were applied to all 1,098 R/S orbit representatives. They produced only **738** distinct signatures, including **222** ambiguous groups containing **621 unordered pairs** of R/S orbits.

Each pair was checked by the earlier complete labeled-action graph correspondence method. For each nonconjugate pair, the source and target full actions were verified transitive. Testing all **243** possible target images of the origin exhausted the complete set of candidate isomorphisms. All 621 pairs were negative: **150,903 candidate origin images rejected, zero missed positive correspondences**. The zero coupling is the one nontransitive 81-reachable-state action and had a unique original four-feature fingerprint, so it did not require the transitive origin-image test.

This second method independently establishes the same 1,098-class count without using the new four-feature complete fingerprint.
