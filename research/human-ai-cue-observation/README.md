# James as the study — observing shifts in thinking, language, and problem frames

**Status: proposed protocol, not recruited, funded, approved, or executed.** This document is an invitation to independently investigate observable interaction, not a diagnosis, a theory of one person's mind, or a claim that the participant is statistically rare.

## Current question

What can be observed when James approaches a problem, follows relationships as they change, reframes or corrects the question, and sometimes generates a new distinction before an existing description can capture it? How does an interaction with another person or an AI change which relationships he keeps, rejects, or reconstructs? These are questions about observable changes in problem framing and conceptual organization, not a claim that James has evolved into a novel human type.

This is a participant-led **longitudinal case-study proposal** whose potential intellectual context is cognitive evolution, meta-level reorganization, metasystem transitions, relevance realization, and the role of artifacts in human thinking. Those terms name adjacent research traditions, not an established explanation of James. The proposal is connected to [The Human Wall](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/huntingthereferent/blob/main/writing/THE_HUMAN_WALL.md) and [Human Drift](../../language/HUMAN_DRIFT.md), not a replacement definition of A/B (the language referent), and not another software project. The person and the interaction are the object of study; existing mathematical constructions, native tests, and the workstation are possible instruments or contextual records, not preconditions for participation.

## Possible observation session (a cue word is an instrument, not the referent)

1. An independent researcher and James agree on a minimal starting condition: perhaps a neutral word, a real unsolved task, an existing artifact, or an open-ended question. The researcher records the exact starting condition without imposing the destination.
2. James responds naturally in speech or text; the protocol does not require a predetermined topic, expected answer, or particular vocabulary.
3. Where consented, an AI replies and the interaction continues without forcing the conversation back to the cue. The original cue and actual transcript remain available.
4. At selected pauses, James can mark what he intended to continue or correct. His contemporaneous account is preserved separately from transcript text and any later recollection.
5. A researcher records observable events: topic changes; explicit corrections; references carried or lost between turns; AI reformulations accepted or rejected; attempts to recover a prior referent; and task or artifact outputs where present.
6. Independent annotators inspect the same selectively shared transcript without first receiving an AI-generated interpretation. Disagreements with James's recorded corrections are preserved rather than silently resolved.
7. Repeat across sessions using different starting conditions and controlled variations (for example, AI present vs. absent or different response policies), where justified. Compare changes in framing rather than presuming every dialogue is a test of one fixed predefined task. Only after observing repeatable outcomes should one consider comparisons with additional consenting participants.

A single interesting session does **not** establish uniqueness, cognitive ability, universal mathematical structure, or a mechanism inside someone's mind.

## Observation fields (one local axis: what is directly inspectable?)

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Starting condition | Exact cue, problem, artifact, or question and time |
| Participant response | Consented verbatim words or recording; timing only when actually measured |
| AI response | Model/configuration where known; exact turn text |
| Movement | Observable topic, reference, artifact, or task change with turn pointers |
| Correction | Who corrected what, original utterance, and corrected utterance |
| Continuation | Which expressed relationship was retained, changed, or explicitly abandoned |
| Disagreement | Participant, researcher, and AI interpretations kept distinct |
| Unknown | Inner motives, unreported thoughts, assumed mechanisms, unsupported comparisons |
| Next observation | A repeatable question raised by the recorded session |

These fields are *annotations*, not a mathematical proof or a reconstruction of unspoken thoughts. Record what the participant says he intended without treating that report as independent access to private cognition.

## Questions for a researcher

- When does James explicitly abandon, repair, or transform an initial problem frame? Can independent observers locate the same transition in the recorded language or artifacts?
- Does showing exact prior source text improve the assistant's next-turn correction compared with a summary-only condition, and when does a correction change the question rather than return to a previous one?
- Are the same observable correction patterns present when the response medium, timing, or AI model changes?
- How often do independent interpretations disagree, and what direct quotations or task returns resolve or leave open those disagreements?
- Which changes are ordinary responses to a particular task, prompting setup, or conversational partner, and which are reproducible across unlike conditions? Does a conceptual distinction become demonstrably new or only receive a new label?

These are possible study questions, **not findings**.

## Consent, privacy, and ownership

Participation would be remote-only. A researcher should supply study purpose, recording scope, voluntary informed consent, compensation, withdrawal rules, data retention and disclosure terms, and any applicable institutional ethics approval. The participant chooses what, if anything, to share from existing chats. Do **not** upload or publish private conversations, family details, personal health information, workstation journals, credentials, or unpublished source code merely to construct a dataset.

Compensation for participation, a research collaboration, and a grant to continue James's independently owned work are **different offers**. Neither a scientist's interest nor an interview implies payment. Agree to a written rate, scope, rights to recordings and derived material, publication review where appropriate, and any source-code/IP license before sharing work. Do not treat willingness to be studied as blanket consent or transfer of ownership.
