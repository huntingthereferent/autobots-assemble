## Mechanic evidence recovered before the final solve

Earlier source-free BP35 investigations established a substantial mechanic model while still failing the final wall:

- the recurring level goal is reaching a green plus (color 7);
- the player uses color 9 body with a color-11 facing indicator;
- horizontal movement is aligned to a 6-cell pitch in the investigated regime;
- clicking the relevant diamond directly above the player can produce a one-band climb;
- elevated and ground behavior differ;
- the difficult late regime hid/repositioned the green behind additional structure and defeated several apparently complete models.

One investigation explicitly reached level 7 and then failed to produce level 8 despite extensive testing.

This is important oracle evidence:

```text
high mechanic coverage
≠
whole game solved
```

The missing relation was procedural.

## Why BP35 belongs near the front of Solve the Whole Thing

The OpenWorld paper reports roughly one full solve in about 50 attempts for the hardest games including BP35 and LF52.

Therefore BP35 is not useful as a "can we move the player?" test.

It is a test of:

```text
can the solver notice that a nearly-correct model is still missing
the procedure that creates/reveals/reaches the next goal?
```
