## Primary correction — A/B is language, not a claim of personal mathematical fluency

A/B is used here as **language for describing, recognizing, and continuing relationships in movement**. It is not itself the same thing as a symbolic mathematical formalism, a theorem, or a notation system. Mathematics is a human formal language that can be used to model particular relations, but those models are translations requiring their own proofs, tests, and limits; they do not replace the observed referent.

The investigator has clarified that they **do not have fluency in mathematical notation** and understand the work primarily through ordinary-language explanations of relations. Research artifacts containing equations, code, classifiers, or proofs must not imply professional mathematical credentials or independent notation fluency. Preserve the distinction between the person's language-first contribution and any formal or AI-assisted technical treatment.

## Motion / material coupling clarification

The investigator calls the explored physical-motion version **couplesense**. It is **not** simply a sensor followed by a controller followed by an actuator. The coupling is itself an interaction: a material relationship is encountered through participation in it, and the interaction changes the conditions of subsequent interaction. A motion realization would need a demonstrable **two-step**:

1. A supported physical movement changes the relation.
2. The result remains admissible to the same operation/contract for a second movement.

The two-step is **not** assumed to require two distinct parts, opposed directions, or an ordinary two-state oscillator. A/B as relational language is not reduced to a mathematical notation, physical device, or workflow. Particular formal models of the two-step remain separate and need their own witnesses.

## Direction, shape, and scale

The investigation concerns **continuing movement**, not maximum instantaneous speed. At scale, many admissible movements may operate concurrently and yield high system throughput without any individual step becoming the fastest. Real physical limits remain.

Lever, crank, engine, tower, circuit board, Earth/egg, grounding, and surface/bouncing descriptions were **communication shorthand and candidate physical contexts**, not definitions or established final designs. No privileged size, shape, medium, direction, or global architecture has been demonstrated.

Possible interchangeable mechanical realizations must preserve the *specified relationship*, not merely resemble one another. Source energy, losses, force, electrical behavior, structural interfaces, and physical evidence remain native engineering constraints.
