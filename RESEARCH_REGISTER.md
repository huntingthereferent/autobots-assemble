Earlier records described a real original SP80 SDK result at **2/6**, then independently verified negative level-2 geometry tests. The October 20 native witnesses advanced that to **3/6**; October 21's genuine native entry advanced it again to **4/6**. Each record remains legitimate within the exact action histories it tested. The [frozen finite-screen contrast](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/blob/702326a0d9c0dc8e9225b4cdd4d9a8c333b778f5/work/arc3/native_sp80_stage2_finite_flow_contrast.py) identified a level-3 action word by evaluating proposals **in the real SDK**, not by assuming the proposed screen-flow model was the engine. Two genuine native histories reached level 3; the [follow-up reproduction](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/blob/702326a0d9c0dc8e9225b4cdd4d9a8c333b778f5/work/arc3/native_sp80_stage3_entry_source_map.py) verified the same entered screen.

The separate [October 20 stage-3 native feedback run](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/actions/runs/39471887855) recorded **32/32 remaining at level 3**. That is still a valid *historical negative result for its tested actions*, but it no longer describes the frontier. On October 21, a [different source-frozen native entry word](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/actions/runs/39616395169) produced **two independent official level-4 returns (2/50 tested native resets), both NOT_FINISHED**. [Subsequent independently replayed level-4 probes](https://github.com/huntingthereferent/autobots-assemble/actions/runs/39636174534) reported **52/52 at level 4 and 0 official level-5/WIN advances**; visual cup changes and source-frame movement were investigated without promoting them to a new level.

## What a research result has to carry

For each investigation, retain the source revision, admitted input, native permitted action or defined operator, returned state or original record, independent observer or verifier (if one exists), counterexample, and the next unresolved boundary.

- **Native game outcome:** the original game's `levels_completed` or `WIN`, never a proxy animation or plausible geometry.
- **Mathematical witness:** an exact declared model, verified computation, assumptions and counterexamples. It does not transfer by analogy.
- **Source-data discrepancy:** original provenance, field meaning, publication-versus-extraction chronology, identifiers and missing versions.
- **Workflow pass:** an outcome of a particular test job at its recorded commit SHA, not a blanket claim about all deployments or all real-world cases.
- **Worker completion:** an admitted actual job and its original journal/owner verification, not the existence of a worker implementation.

Unverified branches and proposals are not silently included as finished work on the source snapshot. Source-owned work can continue; follow a new revision before promoting any newer claim.
