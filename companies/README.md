# Companies

`companies/` contains persistent operating organizations.

A company is not a product, job, capability, or goal lane.

It is a durable referent that may contain:

- operations;
- products and services;
- customers / learners / users;
- people and roles;
- assets and infrastructure;
- finance;
- compliance boundaries;
- evidence;
- work/projects.

A company may begin as an internal operating model before legal formation. Legal status must therefore remain explicit and must never be inferred from the existence of a company record.

## Relation to other layers

```text
company
├─ operations
├─ products / services
├─ work / projects
├─ evidence
└─ external relations

money/products/
→ reusable thing offered by a company

jobs/
→ externally bounded client/opportunity work

work/
→ concrete problems/projects

foundation/
→ demonstrated evidence
```

A company can traverse any of those layers without becoming identical to them.
