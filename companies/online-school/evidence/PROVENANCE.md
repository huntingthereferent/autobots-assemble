# Online School Schedule Provenance

The first operating structure comes from Dessy's existing private homeschool schedule/tracker.

The public company layer carries forward only the reusable operating relations:

- daily rhythm;
- recurring subject blocks;
- instructional durations;
- weekly cadence;
- thematic substitution;
- hands-on project cadence;
- time-tracking structure.

Private learner identities and individualized private records are not copied into the company record.

The source establishes **home-education operating evidence**. It does not establish legal formation, accreditation, licensure, public enrollment, or paid external operation.
