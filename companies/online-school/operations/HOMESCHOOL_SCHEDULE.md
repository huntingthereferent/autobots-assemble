# Dessy Homeschool Schedule → Online School Operating Schedule

## Purpose

This is the first operating schedule for **Online School**.

It preserves the structure of Dessy's existing homeschool tracker while removing identifying minor information from the public company layer.

There are two separate projections:

1. **daily rhythm** — how the day feels and moves;
2. **instructional accounting** — the detailed subject/time schedule used for planning and tracking.

They should not be collapsed into one timeline.

## Daily rhythm

```text
09:30–11:00  school-only block
→ break
→ reading time
→ afternoon activity
```

The detailed subject tracker below is an instructional record and may be distributed/combined across the day rather than fitting literally inside the 09:30–11:00 block.

## Core weekday instructional structure

### Learner A — older elementary track

Daily:
- independent reading — 30 min
- writing — 30 min

### Learner B — early elementary track

Daily:
- phonics workbook — 30 min
- core academic workbook — 60 min
- guided reading with parent/teacher — 20 min
- beginning writing — 20 min

### Shared instruction

Monday–Friday:
- mathematics — 60 min
- science documentary / guided science study — 90 min
- art / creative work — 60 min

Monday / Wednesday / Friday:
- history / social studies — 60 min
- physical education outing — 90 min

Wednesday:
- shared hands-on science project — 180 min

## Math rhythm

The baseline tracker alternates direct mathematics lessons with practice.

Later weekly plans preserve the 60-minute daily math block and commonly use:
- Monday — direct lesson
- Tuesday — practice
- Wednesday — practice / applied work
- Thursday — direct lesson
- Friday — review / practice

Difficulty and method are adjusted to the learner.

## Weekly thematic integration already demonstrated

The existing tracker has already used the same operating structure across different themes:

- **Sep 7–11** — water sources, Missouri River, water cycle, weather, floods/conservation
- **Sep 14–18** — seeds, plant needs, plant parts and related science
- **Sep 21–25** — seasons / fall, sunlight, shadows, seasonal life
- **Sep 28–Oct 2** — trade, stores, packaging, markets, purchasing
- **Oct 5–9** — recipes, kitchen safety, mixtures, yeast, heat, nutrition, food waste

The theme changes while the operating schedule survives.

## Company interpretation

The reusable operating relation is:

```text
stable daily/weekly rhythm
+
learner-level adaptation
+
theme/content substitution
+
tracked instructional time
+
project evidence
→ repeatable learning service
```

The current evidence demonstrates the schedule operating in a home-education context. It does **not** by itself establish accreditation, school licensure, legal business formation, or suitability for unrelated learners.
