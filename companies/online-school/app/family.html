PROVENANCE

What these numbers mean

Saved tasks: currently stored task records. A blank day does not prove no school happened.

Done: tasks marked completed in Online School; not automatically demonstrated mastery.

Planned minutes: schedule estimates, not evidence of teaching.

Actual minutes: entered by an adult on each task. Missing actual time stays unrecorded; marking a task done never fills it automatically.
