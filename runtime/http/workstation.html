How to read the station

Edition means a copy of selected source files. A branch does not inherit its parent's test success.

Ready means a job exists and can be acted upon by an authorized executor. It does not mean a worker is running.

Recorded complete means the runtime saved a result. This viewer does not independently replay that result.

Source changed means the saved job fingerprint no longer matches the visible edition. Historical results stay visible but must not be treated as fresh proof.

Expand Inspect on a job to see its native inputs and recorded outputs. The page does not reverify them. All project-specific mathematical and physical witness standards remain with their native systems.
